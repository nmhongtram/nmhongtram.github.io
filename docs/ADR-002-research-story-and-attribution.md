# ADR 002 — Research story and attribution

**Status:** Accepted
**Decision owner:** Nguyen Mai Hong Tram
**Scope:** Research narrative and public attribution

## Context

The portfolio is for research program admission. Reviewers need to see how the owner's questions evolved and what work the owner personally performed. A conceptual journey can accidentally imply a causal transition that did not happen, or present a team-level outcome as an individual accomplishment without context.

## Decision

- TMA is the point where the owner's interest shifted toward sequential human actions.
- VinRobotics shifted the owner's broader direction toward VLM and VLA research. Its public CV describes temporally grounded action understanding as the work undertaken there.
- Embodied intelligence remains a future direction rather than a completed project.
- For GNN-SurgVQA, the owner reports doing the substantive work from problem formulation through manuscript writing. Public copy should describe that contribution directly without commenting on the other authors' work.
- Structure each short research entry as: question, personal contribution in first person, evidence or learning, then a public link where available.
- Close the Journey with “From recognizing what is visible to understanding what is happening.”
- Do not create a link placeholder for work without a public destination.

## Journey interaction

On desktop, scrolling updates the highlighted stage and a code-drawn conceptual graphic. Visitors can also choose a stage by click or keyboard. On mobile, each conceptual graphic appears with its stage. The graphics are clearly illustrations, not research figures or data. All content remains readable without motion, including under reduced-motion settings.
