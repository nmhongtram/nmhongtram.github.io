# Working glossary

These definitions keep the content model precise during the interview.

| Term | Working meaning | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research work | A sustained question, method, and body of evidence, such as GNN-SurgVQA. | One source record can supply several short views; version one has no standalone research page. |
| Publication | A citable output of a research work, with status, venue, authors, and links. | Accepted but unpublished is not the same as publicly readable. |
| Journey stage | One step in the conceptual and chronological evolution of research questions. | Embodied intelligence is a future direction, not a completed stage of work; show conceptual order without dates. |
| Experience | A role at an organization; it describes responsibilities and context. | Public detail depends on the organization's boundary. |
| Project | A bounded applied or research effort; it may or may not yield a publication. | Version one includes descriptions with links where available; later pages should cover every project and research work consistently. |
| Public evidence | A fact, metric, artifact, or observation approved for open display. | TMA methods and metrics may be public, but data may not. Current CV wording is approved for VinRobotics; its code and labeled data remain private. |
| Canonical work record | One source of truth for a work's public facts and links, reused in context-specific summaries. | A record does not imply a standalone page in version one. |
| VLM | Vision-language model; a stated research direction. | Do not imply this is the only future focus. |
| VLA | Vision-language-action model; a stated research direction. | Do not imply completed VLA research beyond what the CV supports. |
| Contribution | Work the owner personally performed on a research item. | Separate it from team-level results and publication authorship. |
| Evidence or learning | A verified result, artifact, observation, or lesson supporting the research story. | Use only information approved for public display. |
| Concept graphic | A site-drawn illustration of an idea or stage in the Journey. | It is not a research figure, result, source image, or data visualization; label it clearly. |
