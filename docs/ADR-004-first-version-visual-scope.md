# ADR 004 — Text-led opening and conceptual Journey graphics

**Status:** Accepted
**Decision owner:** Nguyen Mai Hong Tram
**Scope:** Visual content in the first portfolio version

## Context

The original product brief proposed a custom hero visual and artifact panels in the Research Journey. The owner prefers a lean first version without imported research figures or project screenshots, while retaining a distinctive Journey interaction.

## Decision

- The opening is text and a primary CV action, without a portrait, abstract hero image, or immediate proof-point highlight strip.
- Version one does not require actual research figures, frames, screenshots, or project images.
- The Research Journey may have code-drawn conceptual graphics that change with the selected or scroll-active stage.
- Label these graphics as illustrations. Do not present them as experimental output, measured data, or actual project imagery.
- Keep Journey text readable when motion is reduced or unavailable.

## Consequences

The visual identity can rely on typography, restrained gradients, an animated progress path, and stage illustrations. Best Paper, rank, and experience evidence remain in their relevant sections. Real figures can be added later without being a prerequisite for version one.
